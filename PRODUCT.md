# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creative directors, brand managers, and hiring leads who are evaluating design talent. They move fast, trust their eye, and decide in the first few seconds whether to keep scrolling. Secondary: agencies and studios looking to bring in freelance creative support.

## Product Purpose

Portfolio for London Carmichael — brand designer, graphic designer, videographer, editor, and photographer. The page exists to demonstrate the breadth of his skills and convert visitors into clients. Success is a new inquiry or booking.

## Brand Personality

Bold, high-contrast, sporty. Confident without being loud. The work leads; the frame is athletic, not decorative.

## References

Nike — sporty, energetic, minimal. Big type, commanding presence, effortless confidence. The aesthetic that strips everything down to essentials and still has explosive energy. Think campaign pages, not product catalogues.

## Anti-references

Swiss/International Style — too rational, too neutral, too grid-obsessed. Pure minimalism that reads as timid or academic. Generic creative-portfolio templates (the white-card-grid-with-hover-overlay cliché).

## Design Principles

1. **The work is the statement.** No ornamental framing. The portfolio pieces carry the weight; chrome gets out of the way.
2. **Contrast is the craft.** High contrast — light against dark, stillness against energy — is the primary design tool, not an afterthought.
3. **Athletic minimalism.** Stripped down but never quiet. Every removed element should increase tension, not reduce it.
4. **Breadth through curation.** Each piece communicates a different skill. The sequence tells a story about range without explaining it.
5. **Move like a campaign.** Pacing, type scale, and space should feel editorial and intentional — more Nike drop than agency case study.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard best-effort. No specific WCAG level required at this stage.
